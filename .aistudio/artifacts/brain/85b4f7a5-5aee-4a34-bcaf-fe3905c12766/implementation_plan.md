## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Provides a completely free, unlocked flight and helicopter radar covering **all UK airspace, all UK airports and heliports, British Waters, West of Shetland, and Norwegian Waters**. Users can track offshore helicopters coming in and out of their platform/FPSO landing zone, monitor HM Coastguard medevac/compassionate flights, check onward commercial flights from any UK airport to get home after crew change, and click any aircraft flying overhead to see its exact model (`S-92A`, `H175`, `AW189`, `Boeing 737-800`, `Airbus A320`, etc.) and where it is flying from and to.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Offshore personnel tracking helideck arrivals, due-off crew-change flights, and onward UK domestic/international connections home, Helideck Landing Officers (HLOs), and anyone spotting aircraft overhead anywhere in the UK.
- **Key Value**: Delivers the complete paid Flightradar experience for free—combining full UK airport/airline tracking with offshore platforms and FPSOs mapped by operator, HM Coastguard SAR tracking, and CAP 437 helideck weather limits.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows
1. **Click Any Plane or Helicopter in the Sky (Offshore or Onshore UK)**: Clicking any aircraft on the map opens the **Live Flight Telemetry Dossier** showing:
   - **Origin → Destination Route**: Explicit **FROM** and **TO** airports, heliports, or offshore rigs, with distance covered, distance remaining, and live ETA countdown.
   - **Exact Aircraft Make & Model**: Decodes ICAO type codes into full human-readable aircraft names (e.g., *Sikorsky S-92A Helibus*, *Airbus Helicopters H175*, *Leonardo AW189 HM Coastguard*, *Boeing 737-800*, *Airbus A320-214*, *ATR 72-600*, *Embraer ERJ-190*, *Boeing 747-8F*).
   - **Full Telemetry**: Airline/operator, flight number, callsign, tail registration, altitude (`ft`), groundspeed (`kts` & `mph`), vertical climb/descent rate (`fpm`), heading (`°`), squawk code, and historical altitude-colored trail.
2. **Track Offshore Helicopters (By Rig Operator or "Due Off" Flight)**:
   - Filter platforms and FPSOs on the map by **Field Operator** (`Apache`, `bp`, `Shell`, `Equinor`, `Harbour Energy`, `TotalEnergies`, `Ithaca`, `EnQuest`, `CNOOC`, `Aker BP`, `ConocoPhillips`) and lock concentric 5 NM / 10 NM / 25 NM approach rings onto your helideck.
   - Or filter by **Helicopter Operator** (`Bristow`, `CHC`, `OHS`, `NHV`, `HM Coastguard`) and **Shore Heliport** (`Aberdeen`, `Sumburgh`, `Norwich`, `Humberside`, `Blackpool`, `Stavanger`, `Bergen`) to watch your due-off chopper leave the beach, land on the rig, and fly back ashore.
3. **Check Onward Flights at Any UK Airport (Getting Home from Shore)**: Open the **UK Airports & Heliports Directory** or click any UK airport on the map to filter live departures, arrivals, and airborne flights around that airport—helping crew coming ashore at Aberdeen, Sumburgh, Norwich, or Humberside track connecting flights across the UK.
4. **Use My GPS / Overhead Spotter**: One click centers the radar on your current location (or any searched UK town/airport) with range rings so you can immediately identify any aircraft flying over your head.
5. **Offshore Weather & CAP 437 Helideck Motion Check**: Live wind speed, gusts, wind direction, wave height, visibility, and barometric pressure at any rig or UK airport, plus FPSO pitch/roll/heave landing limit checks.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Full UK & North Sea Live ADS-B Radar with Dynamic Viewport Polling**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Query `api.adsb.lol/v2/point/{lat}/{lon}/{radius}` dynamically around whatever region, offshore field, or UK airport the user is viewing, enriched with a comprehensive ICAO Aircraft Type Decoder (`S92`, `H175`, `A189`, `A139`, `EC25`, `B738`, `A320`, `AT76`, `E190`, `DH8D`, `B748`, `C17`, etc.), UK/European airport & callsign route resolver, and realistic offshore/regional flight fallback data if an external endpoint is rate-limited.
  - *Why*: Ensures users get smooth, real-time tracking both offshore (West of Shetland, Central/Northern North Sea, Southern North Sea, Irish Sea, Norway) and onshore across every UK airport and town.

- **Decision 2: Complete UK Airports, Heliports, SAR Bases & Offshore Rigs Registry**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Plot all major UK commercial airports, regional airports, offshore heliports, HM Coastguard SAR bases, hospital helipads, and offshore platforms/FPSOs by operator (`Apache`, `bp`, `Shell`, `Equinor`, `Harbour Energy`, `TotalEnergies`, `Ithaca`, `EnQuest`, `CNOOC`, `Aker BP`, `ConocoPhillips`) directly on the interactive radar map and in a searchable directory.
  - *Why*: Lets offshore workers seamlessly transition from watching their chopper land on the rig to checking their return flight to Aberdeen/Sumburgh/Norwich/Humberside and their onward flight home to any UK airport.

- **`HelideckWeatherState`**: `windSpeedKts`, `windGustKts`, `windDirectionDeg`, `significantWaveHeightM`, `visibilityKm`, `temperatureC`, `pressureQnhHpa`, `vesselPitchDeg`, `vesselRollDeg`, `vesselHeaveRateMps`, `cap437Status` (`GO` | `CAUTION` | `HOLD`).
